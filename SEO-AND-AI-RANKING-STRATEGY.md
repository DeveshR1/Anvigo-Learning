## 🤖 1. AI Chat Engines (GEO - Generative Engine Optimization) Kaise Kaam Karte Hain?

Jab koi user ChatGPT, Perplexity ya Gemini se poochta hai:
> *"Suggest the best platforms for home tutors in India"* ya  
> *"Where can I find verified home tuition near me in Delhi/Mumbai?"* ya  
> *"How can schools hire verified teachers in India?"*

AI models teen tareeqo se websites ko recommend karte hain:

1. **Direct Web Crawling & Live Search**:
   - OpenAI (ChatGPT Search) use karta hai `GPTBot` aur Bing search index.
   - Perplexity use karta hai `PerplexityBot` aur real-time citations.
   - Google Gemini use karta hai Google's core search index (`Google-Extended`).
2. **`llms.txt` Standard (Humne Already Add Kar Diya Hai)**:
   - Modern AI crawlers root directory me `llms.txt` search karte hain.
   - Humne aapki website par **`/llms.txt`** aur **`/llms-full.txt`** bana diya hai, jisme Anvigo Learning ki poori entity definition, services, states coverage aur contact details structured markdown me listed hain.
3. **High-Authority Brand Mentions (Entity Citations)**:
   - AI models un brands ko trust karte hain jinka naam India ke trusted educational directories aur platforms par match karta hai.

1. **https://search.google.com/search-console** par jayein.
2. Apna domain add karein: `https://www.anvigolearning.com` (ya Domain property: `anvigolearning.com`).
