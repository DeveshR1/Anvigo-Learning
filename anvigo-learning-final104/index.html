FAQ

Home Tuition, Online Tuition & Teacher Recruitment — Common Questions

Who provides the best home tuition near me?

Anvigo Learning connects you with verified, experienced home tutors near you for Class 1-12, JEE, NEET, Commerce and Spoken English, with a free demo class before you commit.

Does Anvigo Learning offer online tuition as well as home tuition?

Yes. Anvigo Learning offers both home tuition (tutor visits your home) and online tuition (live classes over video call), so you can choose whichever suits your family.

How can a teacher apply for teacher recruitment on Anvigo Learning?

Teachers can register free by filling the Teacher Registration form on the Contact page with their qualification, subjects and address details. Our team reviews applications and connects shortlisted teachers with students and schools.

Can a school hire teachers through Anvigo Learning?

Yes. Schools can submit their teacher hiring requirement — subject needed, budget and location — through the School Hiring form, and Anvigo Learning helps match them with qualified, verified teachers.

Is Anvigo Learning free to use?

Yes, enrolling as a student, registering as a teacher, and submitting a school hiring request are all free on Anvigo Learning.
